## Important
Online product images are not automatically licensed for commercial reuse. Before using a third-party image permanently, confirm that Macrowan has permission or use a manufacturer/distributor asset with suitable reuse rights. Where the exact Macrowan brand/model is unknown, the catalogue should use a generic representative image rather than implying a specific brand/model.
